# Placebo / Falsification Results (2026-08-20)

Design: fake treatment at 2017-01-01 on the 2015-2019 window
(2015-2017 from dedicated placebo exports, 14 files; 2018-2019 from the main dataset).
Same pipeline rules R1-R4. Placebo pairs 2015-2017: 100,032; combined window N = 231,929.

## 1. Lag placebo (log lag, class FE + year FE, clustered class-year)

| spec | N | coef (treated x fake-post) | p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| baseline | 231,929 | -0.066 | 0.459 |
| 10y citation window | 143,501 | -0.085 | 0.284 |

NO placebo effect. The real post-2023 effect (+0.20, p=0.0002 with the same
window) is not an artifact of differential pre-trends.

## 2. Composition placebo: recent-science share (<=3y old papers)

| group | 2015-2016 | 2017-2019 (fake post) | real post-2023 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| treated | 16.1% | 20.1% | 11.9% |
| control | 9.8% | 7.5% | 5.4% |

KEY: fresh-science intake in treated classes was RISING before GenAI
(16 -> 20%) and then collapsed after 2023 (-> 11.9%). The post-2023 freshness
decline is a TREND REVERSAL, not a continuation of field maturation.
This falsifies the "AI field is just aging" alternative explanation.

## Combined reading (preliminary, pre paper-level controls)

- Real DiD: the science-to-patent lag lengthened ~20 log points MORE in
  GenAI-exposed classes post-2023 (robust to truncation guards).
- Mechanism: citation mix shifted toward the older canonical stock
  (2015-2018 deep-learning classics) at the expense of fresh science.
- Placebo: clean on both the lag and the composition margin.
